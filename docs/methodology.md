# Methodology

## Scope
The target is the Slippery-Hill archival catalogue, including tune/recording pages and associated collections.

## Record granularity
A recording occurrence is the basic unit. The same tune performed by different artists, in different years, keys, tunings, or collections remains a separate occurrence.

## Provenance
Original title, artist, key, tuning, year, collection, Slippery-Hill URL, and exposed audio/download URL are retained as source facts. Normalized titles and regional/style descriptions are separate editorial fields.

## Verification
A record is verified from the individual Slippery-Hill page or an authoritative source explicitly tied to that page; search-result snippets alone are not treated as final verification.

## Completeness
Slippery-Hill describes its collection as containing more than 16,000 source recordings. Search results alone cannot prove a complete census, so coverage and verification status must be explicit.
